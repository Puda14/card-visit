# Black & gold digital business card

Separate local Next.js/pnpm site in `black` for Nguyễn Hữu Nhật, based only on the supplied card. The identity is intentionally minimal because no title, company or contact details were supplied. All public data is editable through `.env`; absent channels do not create empty links.

The page uses matte black, restrained gold foil gradients and a locally bundled Vietnamese-capable Dancing Script face for the name. One compact card contains the identity, optional portrait/title/company/bio, phone/email/website/social contact grid, vCard download and profile sharing. English is default with Vietnamese and Japanese options.

The intro behaves like a formal invitation: a black left leaf and gold right leaf meet at the center around an NHN seal, pause briefly, then slide to opposite sides. The page underneath remains stationary. It runs once per tab and supports skip, Escape, focus trapping, reduced motion and no-JavaScript fallback.
